ABOUT//THIS
this is the "hello, i made this" page. it exists so the site has a soul attached to it, and so you know who's reading the guestbook.

i'm slow to reply. it's not you, i am just always sick and exhausted.

hi. i made this.

this is a small personal website to vent, scream, be a mess. allowing the more volatile part of me an outlet. it's a corner of the internet i built for myself, and i left the door open in case anyone else wanted to sit here with me.

everything here was written by studying others, borrowing code, and making it my own. i'm not a professional developer. i just like making things, and i've been doing it long enough that the things mostly work now. kinda. tumblr themeing started it.

nothing here is tracking you. no analytics, no ad networks, no cookies. the only thing that leaves your browser is what you deliberately type into the guestbook or the mood panel, and that's explained a bit further down.

why this exists

because the web got boring

most of the internet is now six websites wearing different hats. everything is a feed, everything relies on algorithms, everything wants something from you. it stopped feeling like a place you could escape into.

the indie web and personal websites are the opposite of that. they're slow, they're weird, they're unfinished, and nobody's grading them. i grew up on those — the fan pages, the shrines, the forums, the sites with a hit counter and a guestbook and a page that says whatever it wants with no explanation. this is me making another one of those.

it also does something practical. the void//log is where i put things so they stop living in my chest. the guestbook is where other people can put theirs. the aid page is a pile of things that helped me, left out in case they help someone else. that's the whole architecture. put it down, and leave it.

the shared bits

  • supabase database for the guestbook and the counters.
  • row level security the database itself decides what's allowed, not the browser.

the look

  • film grain & scanlines made with css gradients, no image files.
  • system fonts no google fonts. for once.
  • reduced-motion if your OS says stop animating, this site listens.

one last thing: if you're reading this and you've been thinking about making your own website — do it. it doesn't have to be good. it doesn't have to be polished or even fully finished. its not for a grade, and the version of the web where everyone has their own strange little corner is a better web than the one where everybody has a profile on the same six sites. steal the layout. view the source. change everything. in the end, use the tools before you. even years back, everyone was borrowing and using code from one another. make it your own, practice it, study it. break it. re-do it. over and over. don't feel ashamed for using a layout generator, or someone else's layout. even i still will use those if i want to. think smarter, not harder.
