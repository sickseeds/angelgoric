brakes — things that interrupt the spiral

temperature

the fastest lever you have. cold shocks the nervous system out of a loop.

  • cold water on your wrists 30 seconds, both wrists, under the tap
  • hold something frozen ice cube, frozen peas, a cold can
  • splash your face cold, not lukewarm. it matters.

breathing

not to calm down. just to give your body something to do.

  • box breathing pacer there's one in the sidebar →
  • guided breathing videos someone else counting for you helps
  • yoga nidra lying down, guided, no effort required

senses

when you've gone somewhere far away and need to come back to the room.

  • 5-4-3-2-1 grounding checklist in the sidebar →
  • asmr, no talking sound without demands
  • deep pressure weighted blanket, tight hoodie, heavy duvet

movement

doesn't have to be exercise. just move a little.

  • ten minutes of stretching on the floor is fine
  • walking in place no leaving the house required
  • dance badly, alone curtains closed is allowed

hands busy

for when thinking is the problem. give the hands a job instead.

  • fold paper origami, or just crumple and unfold
  • draw badly scribbling counts
  • make one thing food, tea, toast. something you can eat after.

one small thing

if everything is too much, don't pick the right thing. pick any thing.

  • spin the wheel sidebar →, one suggestion at a time
  • rain sounds put it on and lie down
  • write it into the void somewhere to put it that isn't inside you
comfort media — things to put in your ears

ambient & endless

no lyrics, no structure, nothing to follow. good for lying still.

  • Music for Airports Brian Eno — the original calm record
  • rain on a window ten hours, if you need ten hours
  • a soft murmur mix your own rain, wind, waves

somewhere else

for when your room becomes too stuffy.

  • radio garden spin a globe, land in a random city's radio
  • window swap look out of a stranger's window somewhere else
  • walking tours follow someone through a city you've never been to

playlists

these are search links, not curated lists. swap them for your own — it'll be better.

  • lofi the reliable standby
  • quiet piano for when you don't want lyrics
  • the sad ones sometimes you need to be met where you are

watch something

low stakes, no cliffhangers, nothing that will ambush you.

  • restoration videos rusty thing becomes clean thing
  • slow tv eight hours of a journey. that's the whole show.
  • neal.fun small weird toys to poke at
reading & listening — other people who get it

organisations

actual charities with actual resources, not influencers.

  • Mind (UK) plain-language guides on everything
  • NAMI (US) family and peer support, lots of writing
  • Trevor Project resources for LGBTQ+ folks, and for people who love them

directories

for finding help that isn't a phone line.

  • find a helpline by country, by topic
  • befrienders worldwide emotional support centres, globally
  • IASP international association — crisis centre listings

words

essays and archives about being unwell, written by people who were there.

  • internet archive old zines, old websites, old everything
  • zine readings people reading their own work aloud
  • podcasts company for a long night

other people's corners

personal sites like this one. the whole point of the web, honestly.

  • neocities browse thousands of handmade sites
  • my void//log you're welcome here
  • the guestbook leave something, read what others left
tools — things that do a bit of the work

tracking

not to grade yourself. just to notice patterns you can't see from inside a bad day.

  • Daylio mood logging without writing paragraphs
  • Finch self-care as a small creature you keep alive
  • Insight Timer free meditations, huge library

make the screen kinder

half of feeling awful at 3am is the light.

  • f.lux warms your screen as the night goes on
  • Dark Reader makes bright sites dark
  • your OS night mode usually already built in, usually off

getting unstuck

for the specific paralysis of having twelve things to do and doing none.

  • a 25-minute timer then stop, whether or not it's finished
  • body doubling someone else working quietly on camera
  • cut it smaller "shower" is really eleven tasks

offline

the ones that work when the wifi is off and you have no phone.

  • 5-4-3-2-1 works anywhere, costs nothing
  • box breathing four counts each way
  • one small thing when choosing is the hard part
a note on this page: i'm not a doctor and this isn't treatment. these are things that have helped me or people i know, gathered in one place so you don't have to go looking while you're already underwater. if something here doesn't help, that's not a failure — it just means it wasn't the right one for you. try the next.

breathing pacer box breathing

four counts in, four hold, four out, four hold. follow the circle or ignore it and just count.
